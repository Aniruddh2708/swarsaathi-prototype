# SR-BENCH-01 — teacher-supervised collection (proposed, QA controlled)
Source: Sprint04 QA master, Benchmark protocol. Adult participants only for primary study. 30 consented adults (10 development, 20 held-out; >=24 beginners overall and >=16 held-out); balance low/mid/high ranges (~6–7 holdout each), multiple tonic pitch classes (prefer >=6). Do not force notes. QA assigns split by singer before recording; all derivatives stay with singer; no holdout labels/audio accessed by developers until frozen candidate/protocol, no tuning on holdout.

## Before session
Obtain institution/team-approved consent for recording, research use, access, retention and withdrawal; no audio in public GitHub or personal cloud/chat. Use pseudonym IDs; consent/contact mapping encrypted separately, restricted to authorized coordinator. Explain voluntary participation, no effect on marks, ability to stop; no names spoken in WAV. Record only adults for this protocol; any minor study requires separate approval and appropriate guardian consent/assent. Define retention/deletion dates and withdrawal process in consent before collection.

## Equipment and calibration
Quiet room, built-in iPhone mic primary, supported second device/route separately; fixed comfortable distance (proposed 20–30 cm), orientation, actual mono PCM sample rate 44.1/48 kHz preferred, no AGC/voice processing if possible, actual settings logged. Do not normalize original PCM. Save lossless mono PCM16 WAV plus original capture if another format; document conversion/no resampling where possible. Record 3s room tone; inspect clipping/dropouts. Teacher and student confirm comfortable Sa in Hz independently of detector; record low/normal/high comfortable notes and conservative range, calibration ID and tuning version, instrument/reference method and uncertainty. Silence playback before scoring/recording. Store actual source timestamps, route, device/OS, app SHA, gain/volume, distance, environmental notes and audio SHA256. No target-derived labels.

## Primary session
For each comfortable supported Shuddha position Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni upper Sa, five takes, ~2s sustained note with ~1s silence between; mark unavailable notes. Teacher provides reference before take, stops reference, waits measured quiet tail, then visual cue. Include comfortable lower/middle/upper registers, two guided phrases at logged BPM, intentional wrong-note and octave examples; no uncomfortable pitch. Add soft and normal voice takes, natural transitions and silent intervals. Keep primary quiet monophonic no accompaniment. Separately record natural fan/noise, hum, speech, tabla/tanpura, playback-only, headphones, speaker and voice-processing routes; do not pool primary. Additional deterministic 30/20/10/0 dB SNR mixtures use original voice/noise RMS over same interval, record seed/hash/clipping; derived copies never cross split.

## Annotation and quality control
Independent F0/voicing tracker plus human review; teacher verifies actual swar, variant, octave, onset/offset, uncertainty, and second reviewer adjudicates. Retain reference Hz and actual label (not planned exercise). QA freezes regular 20 ms grid, full-file unvoiced intervals, steady vs transition eligibility before predictions; uncertainty reason and excluded counts retained; no post-result exclusion. F0 CSV headerless time_seconds,hz (0 unvoiced), entire duration; separate swar annotation CSV with time,voicing,actual_variant,relative_octave,eligibility,reason,reviewer ID. Mark capture faults separately and repeat if consented; retain audit history.

## QA acceptance (provisional)
Exact swar+variant+octave micro/macro >=95%; event passes if >=80% eligible frames correct, event pass >=95%; F0 within50 >=95% including misses; jointly voiced median <=10c/p95 <=50c; missed <=2%, octave <=1%, false voiced <=1%, playback-only valid credit zero. Missing/ambiguous predictions count as failures. Report confusion, per-singer results and 10,000 singer-cluster bootstrap replicates seed 2601; lower confidence bound >=95% is separate unapproved gate. No holdout execution. Do not use Vocadito in holdout.
